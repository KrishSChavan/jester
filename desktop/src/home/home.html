        This Mac’s position
        Where to find a calendar’s link
        Google Calendar
        On calendar.google.com: ⚙︎ › Settings, then the calendar under “Settings for my calendars” › Integrate calendar › Secret address in iCal format. One link per calendar. (A school or work account may have it turned off.)
        Outlook and Microsoft 365
        In Outlook on the web: ⚙︎ › Calendar › Shared calendars › Publish a calendar: pick the calendar and “Can view all details”, Publish, then copy the ICS link.
        iCloud
        In the Calendar app, the calendar’s sharing settings › Public Calendar, then copy its webcal:// link. Anyone with it can see that calendar.
        Canvas
        Calendar › Calendar Feed (at the bottom of the side panel): every course’s due dates, in one link.

        Calendars are read again every 15 minutes, and Google’s link can itself be a few hours behind the calendar. Events a week back to three months ahead are kept: titles, times and places, never descriptions.

        Email

        Gmail He signs in with Google to read your mail and nothing else: he can’t send, reply, change or delete. Of each new message he reads who it’s from, its subject and the opening words Gmail shows in the inbox, never the message itself. You can take his access away at any time here, or in your Google account under Security › Third-party access.

        Models

        A model does two jobs for Jester: talking with you, and actions, working the app in front step by step on commands he doesn’t know (“play Gunna”, “reply to Sam on Slack”), so the names and text of that window’s controls go to it, and a picture of the window when those aren’t enough and you allow it. Tick what each model does, so one can talk and another act without using up each other’s free requests. A job tries its paid models first, then its free ones, each in the order saved, and this computer’s when none of them can: one that fails or runs out is covered for by the next.
