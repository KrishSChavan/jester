Jester

Hand gesture control for web video — all tracking runs on your machine.

Two steps to get going

  1. Click Allow camera access below and accept Chrome's prompt.
  2. Flip Enabled on, open a video, and hold a closed fist ✊ at the camera to pause it. An open palm ✋ starts it again.

Fullscreen vs. windowed

Jester can tell whether a video is filling your screen. Leave this off and every gesture does the same thing everywhere. Turn it on and you get a second set of bindings for when you're not in fullscreen — so a swipe can seek through a film in fullscreen and flip between tabs while you're browsing.

Tabs you're not looking at

Leave this off — the default — and a gesture only ever reaches the tab in front of you. Hold a pose at a Chrome that's minimized, buried under another window or sitting behind the app you're actually using and nothing happens at all, because an action you can't see land is indistinguishable from one that never fired.

Turn it on and those gestures reach the last tab you were using — which is the point when there's a film playing on the other monitor and you only want to pause it. The catch is the same one in reverse: a stray pose caught by the camera while you're working in another application will do something, and you won't see it happen until you come back.

The cursor and the voice pill are the exception either way: both are aimed by eye, so they stay on the tab you can see and come off it the moment you look elsewhere. Sleep / wake Jester is unaffected too — it acts on Jester rather than on a page, so it has always worked with Chrome in the background.

Gestures

Hold a pose steadily to trigger its action. Moving your hand never fires a pose — that's what swipes are for.

Sleep / wake Jester is a full stop, not an off switch. The gesture you bind it to makes Jester ignore everything — poses, swipes, the pointer and the microphone — and that same gesture, and only that one, brings it back. It keeps working even if you change these bindings or go fullscreen in the meantime.

Cursor control

In Both mode, your eyes or head aim and spoken commands click, scroll, or perform a task.

Hold an open palm to show the cursor; close that hand into a fist to click. Head only needs no calibration.

The cursor works inside webpages where Jester has site access.

Voice commands

Bind any gesture to Start / stop voice commands in Gestures above. Pointing up is the default. Hold the gesture once, lower your hand, and speak your full request. A black pill with voice bars appears at the bottom of the page. Pause after speaking to run the command, or repeat the gesture to finish.

Or press Alt+Shift+J (⌥⇧J on a Mac) on any page, Chrome’s own included; change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Beyond video, say things like “open Gmail”, “switch to the recipe tab”, “close all the cooking tabs”, “search Amazon for a USB hub”, “reopen the closed tab” or “open YouTube and play MrBeast’s latest video”.

AI assistant

Say a task such as “Play season 3 episode 1 of Rick and Morty.” Jester follows the page through show selection, seasons, episodes and playback.

Jester uses the first ready model below. When a model is rate limited, it moves to the end and the next ready model continues the task. If all models are limited, a countdown appears on the page until the closest model is ready.

    Add a model

    Free-tier option: gemini-3.5-flash-lite. Create a Google AI Studio API key, enter this model ID, paste the key below, then Add model. Check your active rate limits and free-tier availability; quotas vary by project and can change.

    Enter the API key again to save changes. Saved keys are never shown here.

    Your keys stay in this browser profile and are only sent to their configured providers. Each model used for a task receives your command, your last few commands from the past two minutes, compact relevant page context, task memory and the last few actions — and, only when a request needs them, your open tabs’ titles and addresses or a few matching history and bookmark entries. Simple tab commands (“next tab”, “open Gmail”, “switch to YouTube”) are handled on this device. No raw HTML is sent, and a screenshot only when the setting below allows it. Model API usage may cost money.

    Tasks can open, switch and close tabs and move between sites. When a step needs your OK, the pill asks: say “yes” or “no”, or approve it in the toolbar popup. On Chrome’s own pages, where the pill can’t appear, the question and the answer arrive as notifications and on the toolbar badge.

    Sites treat it exactly like your own input, so hover menus and controls that ignore scripted clicks work. Chrome shows “Jester started debugging this browser” while a task runs; dismissing that bar switches the rest of the task back to scripted events.

    One picture of the tab with every clickable control numbered, sent to a saved model that can read images, at most three times per task. Never taken while a password or payment field is on screen, and never kept after that step.

    Fullscreen

    Chrome only lets a page go fullscreen off a real click, and a hand gesture isn't one. Jester works around that — pick how.

    Feedback & power

    Leave this off — the default — and Jester keeps watching on every tab, so gestures and the pointer still work while you browse. Turn it on to stop inference whenever there's no video to act on, which is easier on the battery. The camera stream stays open either way so it can resume instantly; use the toggle at the top of this page to release the camera entirely.

    Where it runs

    Jester ships with YouTube, Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Prime Video, Max, Peacock, Paramount+, Crunchyroll, Twitch, Vimeo and Apple TV enabled.

    Anything Jester draws on or reads from the page only exists where it runs — the voice pill, the cursor, and voice control of the page itself (clicking, typing, searching a site). Tab, window and playback commands work everywhere regardless, because those run in the extension itself. So if the pill won't appear, or a spoken request can't act on an ordinary site, this is why.
