The first Jester you can download and install, for Mac and Windows.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mac** (Apple silicon, macOS 13+) | `Jester-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| **Windows** (10 or 11, 64-bit) — preview | `Jester-windows-x64-setup.exe` |

Neither is signed with a paid certificate, so your computer warns you the
first time you open it. The guides show the two clicks that get past that:
[installing on a Mac](https://github.com/KrishSChavan/jester/blob/main/docs/install-mac.md) ·
[installing on Windows](https://github.com/KrishSChavan/jester/blob/main/docs/install-windows.md).

Then [Getting started](https://github.com/KrishSChavan/jester/blob/main/docs/first-run.md)
takes about five minutes.

## What's in it

- **No terminal.** Jester downloads what he hears and speaks with himself the
  first time he opens (about 500 MB), with progress in his window. Downloads
  that stop carry on from where they stopped.
- **Permissions when they're needed.** Nothing is asked for up front. The
  microphone is asked for when you turn listening on, Accessibility the first
  time he has to click or type, the camera when you turn gestures on. Asked
  for something he isn't allowed to do, he says what's missing and where to
  allow it, and opens that page of System Settings.
- **Voice first.** A new install starts with gestures off; turn them on when
  you want them.
- **Location that asks.** Set to this computer, macOS is now asked with
  Jester's window in front, where it shows its question. His website asks
  from a button, which is what browsers need.

## The Windows preview

The Windows build is made and started automatically on a Windows machine: it
installs, starts, downloads and unpacks its models, loads the recognizer and
the gesture engine, and its helper for clicking and typing answers. Nobody
has used it day to day yet, so expect rough edges and
[say what you find](https://github.com/KrishSChavan/jester/issues).

Not on Windows yet: linking with the Chrome extension, the Apple Watch, and
Apple's Shortcuts.

## Known limits

- Mac: Apple silicon only.
- After installing a newer version on a Mac, macOS may ask for permissions
  again, and Accessibility may need removing and re-adding
  ([how](https://github.com/KrishSChavan/jester/blob/main/docs/permissions.md#if-a-permission-looks-on-but-doesnt-work)).
- There's no automatic update: a new version is a new download.
